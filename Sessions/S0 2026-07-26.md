# S0 2026-07-26
#session

- Resurrection accessibility? By default in Ekoris, resurrection is not commonly accessible. 
	- This campaign is not meant to be highly lethal/meat grinder style, but Dooms is not planning on pulling punches/fudging to make sure someone WON’T die. 
	- Resurrection “quest” vibe, 2-4 session long side quest that can be completed to possibly resurrection someone. The dead PC would play a backup/temp character as needed. 
	- Some level of narrative dissonance? We can access resurrection magic (via quest), but regular people (even kings) generally can’t. 
- Setting walkthrough
	- New channel! Political Figureheads: four NPCs that control the majority of power in Ashport. 
	- The Leviathan: Deific tier beast asleep in the ocean, bits of it drift off as leviathanspawn. Two cults of the Leviathan: appeasers and awakeners. The more you give up that means something to you, the less Leviathanspawn end up on your shores. In Tasnan, people care about money. 
	- Other organizations! 
		- Temple of Abadar runs most bureaucracy in the city. 
		- “Dockworkers” are shady, associated with criminal organizations. 
		- Former King’s Navy are PIRATES now but the people like them. 
		- Stone Custodians: wild elves who have reclaimed and protect elemental standing stones outside the city
		- The Red Writ: HELLKNIGHTS, looking for an heir to the king, want to restore the “rightful true king of Tasnan” 🙄 Sponsored by Bodin Union so people don’t fuck with them
		- Chroniclers of Rithford: Militant scholars, discover lost history of arcane power in Tasnan. 
	- Other mercenary companies!
		- Golden Fangs: WOLF RIDING HALFLINGS bro that’s so cool. Only hirable caravan escorts per their contract with the Merchant Union. Leader is a former priest of Abadar. 
		- Fire-Bloods: Bad rep, famous for destructive magic + explosions. Don’t care about casualties or damage. One step away from gettin the boot. 
		- Jaques’s Voyagers: Rare travelling Garothan Wight and his ghoul serfs. Commonly referred to as “The Ghouls.” Disliked, have to undercharge to get around bias against undead. 
		- Betan’s Legacy: Recently established knights, bit too foolishly idealistic. Mostly charity work, providing help to those who can’t afford to pay other groups.

- Character creation rules/house rules
	- 20 point buy for stats 
	- Elephant in the room in full use with the exception of non-lethal damage rewrite 
	- Allies within 5' of you do not provide cover to your enemies. Allies further than 5' of you now provide +2 to AC to enemies, rather than +4. 
	- Firing into combat no longer gives a -4 to hit. It instead gives a -2 to hit.
	- Precise shot is altered. Precise shot now removes both the -2 to hit from firing into combat, and causes allies to no longer count as cover for your ranged attacks. 
	- +1/2/3 enhanced weapons are separated from magic weapon augments such as flaming, this will impact the cost of things, but dooms isn’t sure how yet
	- We are using the optional traits/drawback/background skills 
		- Check with Dooms for Campaign traits
	- Scrolls and wands can be used by anyone
