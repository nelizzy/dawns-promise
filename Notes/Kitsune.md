# Kitsune

Also native to [[Ashana]], the kitsune have never been a densely populated race. It is unknown how many kitsune there truly are, as their kind are hidden within other societies. 

Kitsune carry the essence of a demon spirit within them, with some scholars arguing that they are actually a subspecies of [[Tieflings|Tiefling]].

This demonic infusion imbues them with a gift, granting each kitsune the ability to change their form as easily as another changes clothes. 

This bloodline affects not only their physical ability, but also their mind and behavior, and are as drawn towards chaos and malice as the [[Aasimar|aasimar]] is drawn towards righteousness; exceptions exist, but are exceedingly rare. 

A kitsune finds themselves drawn towards lawful societies, an innate pull towards large, structured civilizations to hide within, as they work to dismantle them. 

The devastation that a ninja clan can do if undetected is immense, and cities have been ruined by no more than a few dozen highly skilled kitsune that manage to remain undetected. No more severely is this felt than by the [[Gnolls]] of [[Ashana]], where the majority of kitsune remain, infiltrating into the Great Gnoll Houses, and causing ruin within. 

Even as they work to destroy the civilizations of others, they are also reliant upon them. Kitsune are unable to reproduce with other kitsune, but have the ability to do so with nearly any other humanoid - their offspring always resulting in another kitsune.

Prior to adolescence, a kitsune is indistinguishable in body and mind from their non-kitsune parent's race. 

It isn't until they mature that the young kitsune becomes aware of their own heritage, as they feel the first Call. The Call tempts them to seek out their own kind, and the first gift a kitsune gains is the ability to see through the magical disguises of others, enabling them to locate their kin. 

Requiring practice and training to hone their own shapeshifting abilities, the kitsune form into clans, teaching the young the mystical ways of the ninja, and giving them a safe place to perfect their disguise. 

As this training continues, the Call grows more destructive in nature, and strongly contrasts with the disciplined life within the clan. 

Yet kitsune are never permitted to leave the clan, and only when their skills are honed enough that they are able to escape are they free, ensuring that each kitsune is highly skilled before taking on other societies. 

The Call remains strong for many years, before eventually fading, allowing the most successful of the kitsune to reform back into clans once more, passing their knowledge onto the young.
