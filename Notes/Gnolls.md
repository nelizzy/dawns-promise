# Gnolls

A once brutal species, Gnolls are now renowned for being among the most disciplined people of [[Ekoris]]. 

Many generations ago, when the Gnolls were still living among savage tribes in their ancestral land of [[Ashana]], a [[Nagaji]] ship was destroyed among the rocky cliffs. The sailors survived, able to make it to dry land, and after a display of strength, were able to successfully barter with a nearby gnoll tribe for supplies. 

As the harsh winter began to set in, the [[Nagaji]] continued frequent trade with a number of tribes, and began to teach a more disciplined way of life. The [[Nagaji]] culture of disciplined bladecraft began to spread through the tribes, as the reformed tribes began to unify into a nation. 

Taking the [[Nagaji]]'s discipline even further, they began to restructure their society, living each day by a strict regiment as they seek to attain a zen-like existence, placing great value on overcoming the innate desire for savagery that they possess within. Gnolls today will frequently take solemn vows, with vows of truth, chastity, or silence being some of the most common.

With their people suddenly thriving in a now-cooperative existence, the gnoll's civilization swiftly began to expand, coming into conflict with what is now considered an ancestral foe of the [[Kitsune|kitsune]], who's deceptive nature is antithetical to the modern gnoll, and [[Kitsune|kitsune]] who are discovered to be hiding in gnoll society are swiftly executed.

Gnoll society values the greatest skill of an individual over those with many talents, and Gnolls often will dedicate themselves to a single skill, rather than the balanced martial, magic, and craft of the [[Nagaji]] way. 

The most common dedication for a gnoll will be with the blade wielded by the original [[Nagaji]] captain, the katana, which has now become synonymous with gnoll warriors. 

Today, gnoll society is ruled by a dozen loosely allied Great Houses, although this number rises and falls over time. 

Uniting the Great Houses are three rulers, one a religious leader, one a military commander, and one a bureaucratic administrator, although their power is in contest with the Great Houses, far from being in absolute control of them.
