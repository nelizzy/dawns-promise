## Player Notes
- Animating soulless/unintelligent undead is not cosmically evil, though it is considered vile by most non-Garothians.
- Most animators of undead *tend* to be evil - It's generally illegal and frowned upon.
- Having your corpse raised without a soul does not affect the soul, but rising as intelligent undead *does*. Whether this is evil or not depends on context and consent.
	- Pulling unwilling souls back from the Boneyard is evil in all definitions. Most expect their soul to follow a natural path after death and interference of that is slavery of the soul.
- Outside Garotha, people without formal study of the afterlife/planar energies/etc. are taught that it is evil; reinforced by the fact that most encounters with necromancers are with evil ones.
- TLDR: Necromancy is not inherently evil but is heavily stigmatised and frequently used for evil or by evil people.

- Intelligent undead are seen as generally less evil than tieflings.
- Only mindless undead are immune to mind-affecting effects, so a humanoid vampire can still be charmed.
