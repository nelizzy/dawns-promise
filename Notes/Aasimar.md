# Aasimar

Aasimar are innately called towards good and righteousness. 

The vast majority of Aasimar in [[🌎 Ekoris]] are good aligned, and they are viewed by most as extremely trustworthy, with strangers typically taking their word at face value. 

They tend to be viewed with a mixture of reverence and jealousy. 

The wounds of an Aasimar can be healed from holy water, and being submerged in it can restore life to a deceased Aasimar.
