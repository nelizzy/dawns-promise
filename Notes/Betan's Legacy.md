# Betan's Legacy
A very recently established order of knightly soldiers, considered by some to perhaps be a little too idealistic for [[Ashport]]'s environment. 

Most of their work so far has been charity, helping out with causes that others were unwilling to take on, aiding the poorest of society unable to pay another company. 

[[Betan's Legacy]] stands strongly for the ideals of [[Duke Betan of House Gryffon|Betan]], continuing to champion for change through their deeds and actions.
