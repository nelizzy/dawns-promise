# Darfellan Raiders

Sponsored by [[Eshu Mamuk]] the Crystal Broker

A crystal merchant, importing goods from [[Na-Boa]], has repeatedly caught the attention of a crew of [[Darfellan]] pirates, operating in the most navigable pass northwest of [[The Breezes]]. They seek a company of hired swords to hide below deck, before taking out the raiders once and for all.

## Update 1
[[Eshu Mamuk]] is no longer accepting applicants, apparently having signed a deal with the [[Fire Bloods|Firebloods]] to deal with the pirates.

## Update 2

The taverns are rife with [[Fire Bloods|Fireblood]] braggarts, proudly boasting of saving the merchants of [[Ashport]] as the [[Darfellan]] were butchered by a [[Fire Bloods|Fireblood]] ambush.
