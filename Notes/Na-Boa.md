# Na-Boa

Transitioning out of the frozen lands of [[Astya]], Na-Boa is the ancestral home of the [[Naga]] and [[Nagaji]] of [[🌎 Ekoris]]. 

While there are no great cities in Na-Boa, there are thousands of Temples, each controlled by an individual Naga and her [[Nagaji]]. 

The Naga are strongly independent, although the [[The Bodin Union|Bodin Union]] has staged a coup in Na-Boa, in attempt to secure borders as well as the vast fertile plains of Eastern Na-Boa. While they have succeeded in controlling the much of the plains and installing a loyal Sorcerer Queen, the country remains split, and a large resistance persists.
