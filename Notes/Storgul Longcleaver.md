# Storgul Longcleaver

%% col-start:bc:accent,sb:0 %%

%% col-break:66,stk:1,b:primary %%

A powerful [[Dwarves|dwarf]], formerly a captain in the [[King's Army]]. He now commands a great deal of power within [[Ashport]], and is the strongest individual military power in the city, between his clan of [[Dwarves|dwarven]] kin and the soldiers loyal to him from the King's Army. 

He is viewed as a just and honorable man by the majority of the city, and was one of the earlier influential members of the military to side with the revolution, earning him enough goodwill to surpass the distrust many have of [[dwarves]]. 

He controls the [[Ashport Militia]].
