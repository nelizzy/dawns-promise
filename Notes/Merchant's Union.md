# Merchant's Union

The [[Merchant's Union]] is a mandatory guild for all traders bringing goods into or out of the city. 

The [[Merchant's Union|Merchant Union]] also funds the guards in the warehouse and market districts, who not only protect the property of the Union, but also ensure that any trying to operate without paying dues are met with unfortunate business conditions.

They have a contract with [[The Golden Fangs]] for exclusive protection duties.
