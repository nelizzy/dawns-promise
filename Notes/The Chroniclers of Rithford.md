# The Chroniclers of Rithford
A group of scholars, as well as their hired swords, having tasked themselves with recording the lost histories of [[Tasnan]], with a heavy focus on locations of arcane power and importance.

While the scholars themselves are rarely skilled in martial combat, they have a greater wealth of arcane knowledge than most, and are able to bring such might to bear when necessary.
