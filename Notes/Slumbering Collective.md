# Slumbering Collective
While in control of the [[docks of Ashport]], [[Crimson Serenity]] and the [[Slumbering Collective]] do not enforce most common laws of the city, allowing the [[Ashport Dockworkers United|United Dockworkers]] to operate freely within their territory. Rather, their focus is on any other cults or worship of the [[Leviathan]], rooting out any who diverge from the Collective's doctrine.

The [[Slumbering Collective]] is a large cult to the [[Leviathan]], taking a tax from every outgoing ship from [[Ashport]]. Gold and treasures are routinely taken out into the deepest waters in the center of [[🌎 Ekoris]], and thrown overboard to pacify the [[Leviathan]]. 

The [[Deep Speakers]] are the militant wing of the [[Slumbering Collective]]. These warriors zealously train in preparation for encounters with [[Leviathan Spawn]]. Capable of the [[Depth Speech]], they typically are able to ward off spawn with negotiation & sacrifice, but each Deep Speaker also carries a long pike, able to pierce the tough hide of what dwells beneath the waves. They keep interaction with the citizenry of [[Ashport]] to a minimum, and the common man views them with fear due to their connection to [[Leviathan]], even as they respect the safety they provide from the deeps.
