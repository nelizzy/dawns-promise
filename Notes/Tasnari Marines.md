# Tasnari Marines
While most of the [[King's Navy]] has disbanded, a few ships still call the docks of [[Ashport]] home. 

These ships claim independence, but tend to act cooperatively, and it's commonly known that they have turned to piracy when the royal treasury was seized. They don't interfere with trade coming to or from [[Ashport]], and are not viewed negatively by most of the locals. 

The [[Merchant's Union|merchant's union]], however, has a strong distaste for them, with the marines often undercutting prices below that which a legal business could attain.
