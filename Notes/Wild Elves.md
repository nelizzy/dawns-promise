# Wild Elves

Wild Elves, or Wode Elves, or Wood Elves, are the second most prevalent. 

Wild Elves tend to be less adjusted to civilization than [[Sun Elves]], with most preferring their own, isolated communities of hunter / gatherers. 

Wild Elves tend to have extreme emotional swings, easily passing from friendly to hostile at a perceived insult or trespass. They have a strongly mercurial nature, and are often feared by commonfolk. Unpredictable, they will often allow dozens of caravans and travelers to pass through their lands, before violently attacking the next, for trespassing. 

Wild Elves are cannibalistic by culture, and eating the dead is a sign of respect, allowing the fallen to live on in your own strength. There is no insult more grievous to their kind than 'Ghoul Elf', and to utter such words will result in death or a lifelong enemy, making new enemies from each tribe that knows of your insult.  

Wood Elves live slightly longer than [[Humans|human]] lifespans. 

Wild Elves make armor and weapons from bone, leathers, and hide. Their only heavy armor is made from [[Dwarves|dwarven]] bone, being strong enough to provide heavy protection, while being light enough to stay moveable. 

Additionally, Wild Elves are naturally resilient to the [[Dying Curse|Dwarven Death Curse]].
