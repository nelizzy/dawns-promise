# The Last Lanterns
- Ex-[[military]] of [[Ashport]], fought on the [[revolutionaries]] side of the war.
- Led by [[Varkash Dren]]
- Held the rearguard of the army, using signal lanterns to cover retreats.
- Post revolution, they held [[Keep Clearwater]]
	- [[Varkash Dren]] had been replaced by a black smoky demon that controlled their minds, turning them into robbing brigands.
- After the defeat of the demon, the surviving Lanterns joined [[Dawn's Promise]].
