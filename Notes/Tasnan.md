# Tasnan

A land of gentle hills and many rivers, Tasnan has many coastal cities and a strong trading presence, with a very stable neighbor of [[Garotha]] on the Eastern border. 

In the Southern mountains, [[Orcs|orc]] tribes live in the frozen heights, sweeping down into Tasnan cities for savage raids. The Western forests are home to many [[Wild Elves|Wild Elf]] tribes, who have always been on tenuous terms with the rest of the [[Humans|human]] dominated landscape. 

Within the civilized areas of Tasnan, the Tasnari people had been suffering under the oppressive rule of an autocratic ruler, with conditions worsening with each passing year, while the nobles grew richer and more opulent. 

One man continued to give hope to the people, [[Duke Betan of House Gryffon]], was a powerful and beloved political figure, well known for standing up for the common people. Fighting within the courts to reduce taxation, increase wages, and strengthen the rights of citizens, he was also famous for the creation of a public education system, bettering opportunities for those without the funds for tutors. When he passed from a sudden illness, many of his reforms were repealed as soon as word of his passing reached the court, and a wave of outrage from the masses swiftly followed. 

This outrage sparked the flames of revolution, and the following several years of conflict saw some of the most reprehensible atrocities [[🌎 Ekoris]] has known, before eventually seeing the execution of the king, the royal family, and most of the noble caste.

To call it a victory, however, would be a hollow phrase. Years of violence and death have jaded many of the once bright-eyed [[revolutionaries]], and many lost the very people they were fighting so ferociously for. 

Precious few have made it through the revolution without losing friends and family, and all their efforts for a better tomorrow have instead seen the rise of warlords, merchant princes, and militia kings fighting over same land that they once shared a vision of. Most have lost the belief that Tasnan will ever be the land they once dreamed of, having since accepted the brutal nature of life in Tasnan as an inevitable truth. 

A common saying in Tasnan to dismiss a complaint is "Justice was buried with [[Duke Betan of House Gryffon|Betan]]," and the common cause that once was shared has long since vanished. 

People in Tasnan are selfish by necessity, with the notion commonly accepted that you'll only get out of life what you can take.
