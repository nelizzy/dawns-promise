# Tieflings

Tieflings are fiendspawn, and their fiendish heritage strongly influences their behavior. 

The mere touch of a tiefling is highly painful to humanoids, although will not leave any lasting harm. 

The vast majority of tieflings are evil, prone to violence and reveling in suffering. The life for the rare exception to the rule is incredibly difficult, as they will encounter hostility, violence, and rejection until they prove themselves unlike the rest of their kind, and having overcome their fiendish nature. 

Tieflings have an innate bonus when fighting [[Aasimar]], easily able to inflict deadly wounds.

## Player Notes
- Tieflings in humanoid society are very slightly better regarded if they wear gloves, as it spares people the painful touch (equivalent of muzzling a scary dog)
- Seen as worse than intelligent undead, but better than kobolds and kitsunes
- An average tiefling is generally closer to adventurer level than an average humanoid (is this the same for Aasimar?)
