# Ashport Militia

Lead by [[Storgul Longcleaver]].

The most numerous of the forces within [[Ashport]], the militia serves to enforce general law and order throughout the city. They are controlled by a council of 5 [[Centurions]], stylized after the [[The Bodin Union]], though in practice the other four vote in tandem with [[Storgul Longcleaver]]. Formed both from soldiers of the old [[King's Army]], as well as [[revolutionaries]], they share common cause in care for the city and its people, wishing to see [[Ashport]] spared from continuing mayhem and destruction. 

The militia has three main divisions within. The first is a group of lightly armored watchmen, tasked with patrolling the city, trained with saps and sword alike. 

The second, a guard regiment who posts at key positions throughout the city. These guards are more heavily armored, and proficient with crossbow and halberd. 

Finally, they have response teams, more varied in equipment, able to be summoned to deal with major threats.
