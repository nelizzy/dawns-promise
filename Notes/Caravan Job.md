# Caravan Job

Sponsored by [[Twin Moon]]

This job is brought discretely to [[Dawn's Promise]] by an agent of Twin Moon, a rather successful [[catfolk]] merchant who imports various goods from [[Harath|Harathi]] caravans, then sells throughout the region of Northern [[Tasnan]]. 

He tells you that the extortionist rates [[the Golden Fangs]] charge serve only to enrich the leaders of the [[Merchant's Union]], leaving those actually delivering goods across [[Tasnan]] to suffer the burden. He plans to raise a motion to overrule [[Roquill of the Seven Pines|Roquill]]'s exclusive contract with [[the Golden Fangs]], but requires an inciting incident.

He requests a robbery, of his own caravan. Disguise yourselves as bandits, and prove that the service provided by [[the Golden Fangs]] is unworthy of their fees. He also requests discretion, should you choose not to accept this offer.
