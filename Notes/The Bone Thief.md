# The Bone Thief

Sponsored by [[The Gilded Compass]]

Cemetery plots are being unearthed in the night, in the nearby small village of [[Pinecrest]]. [[The Gilded Compass]]  has put out a notice, offering a very small sum as a reward for investigation and stopping the thefts. The greater reward would certainly be winning favor from the Church.

## Update 1
With a letter delivered back to [[the Gilded Compass]], you remain on guard of the location for another day, before a ranking priest arrives with a small entourage, assuring you that they will take up a vigilant watch over the [[Ossuary]] and the necrotic [[Wellspring]] that lies within. While fully closing a [[Wellspring]] is a tremendous task, placing wards to contain it and guarding the location is far more manageable. They inform you that a barrier will be enacted, which will contain the presence of [[Nineteen Claws Ebb Slumber]] to within the [[Ossuary]] as well, preventing it from reaching out to any passersby. 

You are thanked for your efforts, and while not explicitly stated, there is a clearly implied undertone of an owed favor for your assistance in the matter. [[Dawn's Promise]] is rewarded the agreed amount of fifty gold.

A few days later, back at the guildhouse within [[Ashport]], you receive a visitor. A man introducing himself as a representative from [[Jaques' Voyagers|Jaques's Voyagers]] says that he's heard you've discovered something interesting in a recent job near [[Pinecrest]], and inquires for more information. 

---

It seems the consensus is that while we acknowledge something beyond the job description _was_ found, said discovery has been reported to and is now under the care of [[the Gilded Compass]]. Should the Voyagers wish for any details about the discovery, they should direct their inquiries to [[the Gilded Compass]]. :)

We would also, then, let [[the Gilded Compass]] know of the inquiry from [[Jaques' Voyagers|the Voyagers]].


## Update 2
After some times passes, [[Baron Jaques]] and his [[Jaques' Voyagers|Voyagers]] make a play to secure guardianship of the [[Necrotic Wellspring]]. 

Working through your contacts at [[the Gilded Compass]], you have shut down the ploy, preventing the wight and his ghouls access. Better yet, you've done so in a way that does not tie back to you being the obstacle in his way.
