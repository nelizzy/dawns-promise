## Player Notes
- Many different origins of Lycanthropy exist from mundane disease to magical curse [^1]
- The origin decides what the Lycanthrope is weak to (e.g. silver, fire)
- The longer one is cursed, the more affected by the curse they are
	- Damage resistance increases, lycanthrope weakness increases
	- Corrosion of the mind that turns them into feared monsters
	- Regeneration is a late stage power of the curse, increasing in potency
- When a lycanthrope dies, they return to their true form — what they were before they died
- Lycanthropes have three forms: full wereform (usually the animal they become), true form, and hybrid form (can be mistaken for hybrid animal races like ratfolk or catfolk)
- Animals can also be infected with lycanthropy, gaining increased sentience and speech (see [[Squin]])
