# The Breezes

Although officially called The [[The Breezes|Kingdom of Otha]], referring to it as such locally will mark you as a foreigner, it instead locally being known simply as The Breezes. 

Theoretically controlled by [[King Takasi]], in practice he is a meek man with neither the power nor means to control more than the immediate land around his castle. True power is held by the [[Sea Lords]].

The Breezes, while not barren, has no bounty of natural resources, no massive population used to swell numbers, nothing that would be of major benefit to the [[The Hassan Dynasty|Hassan Dynasty]], nor the [[The Bodin Union|Bodin Union]]. 

Combined with the highly mobile and flexible power of the Sea Lords, The Breezes simply aren’t worth attempting to control for either of the major powers, for fear of driving the Sea Lords to the other power, while only actually controlling a section of near useless, backwater land.
