# The Golden Fangs
A [[halfling]] centric mercenary company, famous for their wolf riders. 

They have a contract with the [[Merchant's Union|Merchant Union]], mandating The Golden Fangs as the only permitted hirable caravan escort outside the city walls, although most members of the Union despise the rates they are forced to pay for protection. 

They also have a partnership with the [[The Gilded Compass|Gilded Compass]], with the captain of the Fangs being a former priest of Abadar.
