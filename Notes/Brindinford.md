# Brindinford
#location/poi #location/town #tasnan

It used to be considered a main cultural center of [[Tasnan]], before the revolution. 

Since then, it's rebuilt up as a moderately large town with a reputation for attempting to bring back the arts and performances to a devastated Tasnan, ruled by the noble blooded [[Baron Euphemes the Second]]. 

The Baron has a reputation for successfully employing bread and circus techniques to maintain power, using the considerable wealth of his fallen noble brethren to buy the loyalty of Brindinford. 

Some of these funds have gone to attracting travelling events, including the inbound faire mentioned in [[A Faire's Ill Portents]].
