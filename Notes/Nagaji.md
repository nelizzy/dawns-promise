# Nagaji

The Nagaji are a proud people, native to the country of [[Na-Boa]]. Their artisans and artists are well respected, along with their famed skill at arms - martial traditions being a strong mark of their heritage. 

The Nagaji also have a long history of powerful sorcery, and it is rare to find a Nagaji without at least some talent for arcane magic. Every Nagaji is expected to attain both martial mastery with a chosen armament, as well as mastery of a craft.

Nagaji are hatched within a Temple, in service to the Naga within. Very rarely does a Nagaji strike out on their own, instead staying faithfully and serving their Naga. A Nagaji nearly never will serve a second Naga, and if the one they are born to dies or is otherwise unfit to follow, the Nagaji will find a new path, held to no master.

Most famed among them are the Temple Guardians - those who have been trained in ancient Nagaji glaivecraft, blending sweeping cuts with arcane strikes. Such warriors swear guardianship to their Naga, a lifelong oath. Should they fail in their oath to protect their Naga, and the Naga is slain, the Temple Guardians will ritually commit suicide to continue service in the afterworld, their spirit tethered to the Naga by binding oath.

Common longer Nagaji weapons will be made of wood with cutting points from the fangs of beasts, with smaller weapons not needed for parrying made entirely from glass. 

Common armors are typically made from reptilian beast hides. 

A highly respected profession among the Nagaji are the Crystal Whisperers, able to very slowly shape the growth of crystals through song. These vibrant and varied colored crystals are diligently shaped over years into fitted armor and weapons, and are wielded by favored Nagaji and the Temple Guardians.
