# Greed Sickness
A [[Dwarves|dwarf]] that acquires more wealth than is immediately necessary for a common standard of living becomes at risk. 

Roughly 10-20% of [[Dwarves|dwarves]] are affected at that point, with an increasing % as more wealth is acquired, although some never fall prone to the affliction. 

Once it begins, there is no known way to reverse the corruption, as it steadily builds into an all-consuming desire, deepening with each coin horded, rending away any goodness that may have been within them, replaced entirely with a need for more. 

More than a mere miser, an afflicted [[Dwarves|dwarf]] who has heavily fallen into the sickness will do anything to keep but a single copper coin from falling from their grasp, no matter how severe or cruel it may be. 

Because of this, roughly a third of total [[Dwarves|dwarves]] swear vows of poverty, living as meager existence as they can muster, in order to stave off the possibility of this corruption.
