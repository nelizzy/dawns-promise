# Dawn's Promise

A mercenary company stationed in [[Ashport]], headed by [[Laurel]], [[Noel]], [[Sir Roland]], [[Sir Taymour]], [[Varka]], sponsored by [[House Baramber]].
## WHO ARE WE
- Dawn’s Promise is sort of a baseline foundation to a decent chunk of contracted people. 
- Larger than a crew but smaller than a full-fledged army, made up of a number of grouped, smaller units. 
   - If (highly specialized, expensive is 1 person) and (rank and file, large but cheap army is 10 people), we're like... 4-6? :kek: 
- We recruit common fighters and specialists, give them a baseline kit and minimum training, then form them into small squads that train together and develop from there, often with a proven squad leader? 
- Equipment allowances or w/e to help them improve their kit to their own needs.
- The goal isn’t to stamp out identical soldiers, but to multiply each person’s strengths and build teams that fight cohesively in their own style.  
- Hands-off: contractors work on commission, often under repeat contracts, and are free to live as they like so long as they honor the terms. 
- Individuals are expected to improve and add to their kits so they can do better at what they were pulled on for.
- Squad cohesion > uniformity. 
- Once you take a contract, you see it through, or you answer to Taymour.

## WHAT OUR NUMBERS ARE
- 60ish people
- 20% non-combatants (scouts/messengers/administration/crafters/etc.)
- 70-75% combatants (your bogstandard non-magical combatants -- shields, spears, swords, etc.)
- 5-10% magical (combatant and support alike - i feel like we try to bring in mages where we can but have less support for it than a purely magical guild)

## WHAT WE'RE KNOWN FOR
##### *Clients, why hire us?*
- written contracts, 
- steadfast and reliable, 
- jack-of-all-trades work coverage with leaning towards standard security
##### *Recruits, why join us?*
- steady employment and pay, 
- support in development (training, kit, etc.), 
- meaningful work,
- loyalty of the leaders (if someone fucks with you unjustly, they'll find the fuck out)
