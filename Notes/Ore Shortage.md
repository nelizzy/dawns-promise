# Ore Shortage

Sponsored by [[Doran Northlander]], the primary purchaser of South Creek Mine ore.

The ores coming from the [[South Creek Mine]] has slowed in the past month. Reportedly, accidents both in and out of the mine are becoming common, and the locals have been acting strangely. A collection of weaponsmiths are all owned by [[Doran Northlander]], one of the more influential [[dwarves]] beneath [[Storgul Longcleaver|Storgul]]. These smiths are operating at less than half efficiency due to lack of material - and Doran is offering both gold and armaments to someone who can solve the problem and return the mine to normal operations.

## Update 1
Doran Northlander raises the reward for resolution, with his fury becoming quite public at Baron Jacques and his Voyagers for abandoning the job in the middle.

400 Gold, +2 Military Capital -> 600 Gold, +3 Military Capital

## Update 2

After Jacques and the Voyagers are thwarted in their attempt to secure the necromantic wellspring, they return to attempt to continue the work for Doran Northlander, and the furious argument can be heard all up and down the street. Their contract is refused, and Doran escalates the payment further still for any others willing to accept. 

It is made clear that any who accept Doran's offer will earn the ire of the Voyagers, who wish to see Doran fail and be forced to sell his ownings.

600 Gold, +3 Military Capital -> 800 Gold, +3 Military Capital, +1 Bureaucratic Capital
