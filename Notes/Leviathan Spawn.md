# Leviathan Spawn

Spawn of the [[Leviathan]].

# Types of Leviathan Spotted

1. Serpentine blue goo creatures [^1]
	- Their aura displaces people that approach, teleporting them away—though the creature can choose to follow them.
		- Fort/Will (lowest of two) save
		- On success, you choose the highest of two going forth.
		- On second success, you ignore the aura going forth.
	- Wet, sticky tails that grab onto a creature, leaving their head free to attack others.
		- While grappled, always lowest save against displacement.
	- When defeated, they return to the blue goo.
	- Time seemed frozen around them, uncertain if unique to the area or creatures.
