## Player Notes
- Wellsprings are places of powerful magic formed when planes bleed into each other
- [[Spirits]] can come to live in one and make pacts with a "pactkeeper"
- Materials gathered from wellsprings are used as magical components and often give tinges of their presence e.g. [[Brindinford]] tends to use a wellspring to the south that gives their magical ink a green tinge (see [[Magic System and Scrolls]])
