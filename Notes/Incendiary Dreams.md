# Incendiary Dreams

Sponsored by [[Tarni]], Priestess of the Temple of Desna

A warehouse in a manufacturing quarter of [[Ashport]] burnt down in an accident about 4 months ago. The survivors have frequently suffered nightmares of the fire, of being one of those unable to escape. While at first written off as mere trauma, nearly identical nightmares have begun to haunt those who have recently been hired to rebuild the warehouse. Find the cause and put a stop to it, and allow the warehouse to be rebuilt.

## Update 1 
No other company has claimed this task, and the dreams have continued. Workers have stopped all construction, and the man originally rebuilding the warehouse seems to have focused his construction efforts in other areas of [[Ashport]]. The survivors continue to be forced to endure nightly trauma, and the Desnan Priestess finds her talents stretched too thin to aid them all.
