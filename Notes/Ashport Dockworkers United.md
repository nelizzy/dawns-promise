# Ashport Dockworkers United

Originally formed about twenty years ago as a collective bargaining force for the dockworkers, the docks now serve as the home to a wide network of organized crime dealing with business both in and out of [[Ashport]]. 

While the [[Ashport Dockworkers United|Dockworkers]] used to be a respectful profession, most of those still in the job are affiliated in at least some manner with the unsavory side of the docks.
