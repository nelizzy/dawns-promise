## Player Notes
- A lopsided tower in [[Brindinford]], [[Westhill]] that sticks 50ft above its neighbors
- Difficult to spot - there seems to be some sort of memory manipulation magic around it, causing you to forget about it unless you are directly looking at it
- Exists in odd space - hex-map.
- Once home to a [[Leviathan]] [[awakeners]] cult and kept an assortment of Leviathan lore, reagents, and a bone quill on the upper levels.
