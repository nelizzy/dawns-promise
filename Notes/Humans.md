# Humans

Humans are the most widespread race across [[🌎 Ekoris]] at large, having a large variety of cultures across the continent. Humans do not tend to have a strong cultural identity from race, but draw their culture instead from their region. 

Humans are gifted with the Secrets of Metal, and are the only species capable of smelting metal - an [[Elves|elf]] or [[Dwarves|dwarf]] that tries the same technique will merely cause brittle, unshapable ore.
