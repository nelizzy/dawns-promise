# Isle of Rivers

The Isle of Rivers, named for the thousands of rivers crossing down from the mountain, is home to the vast majority of [[Star Elves]] on [[Ekoris]]. 

Outsiders are not permitted, and many of the commonfolk of [[Ekoris]] resent the [[Star Elves]] for not aiding in problems that they perceive beneath them. The Isle of Rivers is rumored to house grand libraries, and to be the greatest store of magical knowledge on [[Ekoris]]. 

While their power is not considered to match that of the [[The Hassan Dynasty|Hassan Dynasty]] or [[The Bodin Union|Bodin Union]], neither faction is willing to pay the high toll that conquest of the Isle would take.
