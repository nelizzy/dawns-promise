## Player Notes
- Scrolls activate based on intent - you do not need to understand the scroll, but you *must* be willing to activate it [^1]
- A used scroll loses its magic - you cannot copy spells from used scrolls. How this appears depends on the scroll; some burn to ashes, some fade the ink to nothing, some blot over completely
- Incanted rituals need a significant source of power, like patrons or a [[Wellspring]]
- Magical inks share properties based on where its materials are harvested from - e.g. the [[Leviathan]] ink is purple and seems to flow like a river, while local [[Brindinford]] inks tend to source from the same wellspring which gives a green tinge
