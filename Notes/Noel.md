# Noel

- A Founder of [[Dawn's Promise]]
- Defected from a [[revolutionaries]] group that eventually became the [[Fire Bloods]]. 
- Father is [[Torvald Bjornson]] and mother is [[Alice Bjornson]]
- Grew up near the [[docks of Ashport]] and despite everything has some fond memories from there; holds more reverence for [[appeasers]] than most, due to proximity to the water.
