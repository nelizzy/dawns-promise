## Player Notes
- The local [[appeasers]] faction of [[Brindinford]] [^1]
- They take tithes and get a set cut from the town's coffers.
- Led by [[The Voice]] [^2]
- They have a special ritual when joining; unlikely to betray the cause?
- They make use of Abadar truth glyphs.
- Live in a tower by Brindinford's southern docks.
	- They cannot leave the tower undefended as there is some artifact within that cannot be left unprotected.
- Recognizable by the classic faceless masks and wearing silver.
- Safeword "Serendipity"
- Use long pikes and crossbows as weapons.
