# Astya

Largely a frozen, inhospitable wasteland, Astya is home to many tribal [[orcs]] & [[goblinoids]], constantly warring with one another. 

While Astya has had valuable mining expeditions & outposts in the past, all have fallen in the tribal wars, and the wealth beneath the land goes untapped for now. 

Not too much recorded history is known about Astya, as the native inhabitants tend to be of oral traditions, and rarely migrate to other lands.
