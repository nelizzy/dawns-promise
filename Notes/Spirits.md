# Spirits

...

## Player Notes
- Spirits are supernatural entities *beyond* just the souls of the undead, varying in strength and type
	- Many have elemental/planar energies associated with them like fire, water, positive, negative
	- Think like Spirit Island style spirits / spirits from Shamanism
- They are not explicitly tied to [[Wellsprings]], but are more likely to be found by one

## Known Spirits
- [[Nineteen Claws Ebb Slumber]] found in the [[Necrotic Wellspring]] in [[S3 2026-08-30]], last seen there
- [[Petals in a Stilled Sea]] found in a burned warehouse in [[S5 2026-09-27]], currently in [[Sir Roland|Roland]]'s body
- [[Roland's Positive Spirit]] source of Roland's positive energy, currently in [[Sir Roland|Roland]]'s body
