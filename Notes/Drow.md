# Drow

Drow, or Cavern Elves, are native to the land of [[Harath]].

Although many live deep within the earth, there are also rare, yet often affluent drow surface communities. 

Drow power is held in a tyrannously tight gasp by matriarchal priests. 

Drow rarely worship deities, tending to instead favor the worship of powerful outsiders. 

The drow are also renowned masters of animal husbandry, with many drow prominent families earning vast fortunes from breeding and selling beasts of labor, war, or food. 

The whip is central to drow culture, considered an extension of the wielder's hand, and commonly carried to guide beasts, to serve as a weapon, for communication, and as symbol of status. 

Drow are not an innately evil race. 

Drow live slightly longer than [[Humans|human]] lifespans, although some live many times longer through favored pacts with outsiders. 

Drow typically trade with [[Humans|human]] cultures for metal goods, although when they lack the ability to do so, they tend to use leather and hides for armor, with beasts serving to make up much of their physical might.
