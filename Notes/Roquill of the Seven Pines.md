# Roquill of the Seven Pines

%% col-start:bc:accent,sb:0 %%

%% col-break:33,b:primary %%

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/sk0qaemk/image/upload/v1791289467/jqqgc05eg5jic0czzdnv.png)

%% col-break:66,b:primary %%

A [[catfolk]] merchant from the [[The Hassan Dynasty|Hassan Dynasty]], who has gained vast fortunes from trading exotic spices. 

Rouquill now wields vast power through wealth and influence, and little trade of significance happens in the city that he is not aware of. Rouquill has been shrewd enough to keep the masses happy, sponsoring the [[Ashport Arena]] and is generally viewed favorably by the common people, who consider him to be the reason for access to most goods in the city.

While he does not rule the [[Merchant's Union]], he is the most individually influential member of it, and much of the policies enacted favor large, established traders such as himself.
