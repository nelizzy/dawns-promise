# Jaques' Voyagers
Lead by one of the exceedingly rare [[Garotha|Garothan]] Wights that travel outside their country, [[Baron Jaques]] has brought with him a group of loyal serfs, as well as a band of ravenous ghouls under his command, and established a mercenary company in [[Ashport]]. Viewed with fear and suspicion from the common folk, [[Ashport]] does not have the same honorable opinion of the walking dead as [[Garotha]]. 

[[Baron Jaques]] is willing to under-charge for his services to help get around the bias against his kind, and has proven both a capable warrior himself, and an effective commander, leading man and ghoul alike. 

While their official company name is Jaques's Voyagers, most refer to them as merely The Ghouls.
