# The Hassan Dynasty

The other major power of [[🌎 Ekoris]]. 

An absolute monarchy, the dynasty does not represent a typical line of lineage. 

Each King swears an oath to take no family and sire no children, and royal [[Samsaran]] prophets determine a child to be adopted to serve as prince, who will be reborn in a ritual, taking the name [[Hassan]]. Hassan is considered a sacred name, and it is a crime punished by death to name a child as such. 

As a nation, they do not possess the same military might as the [[The Bodin Union|Bodin Union]], but have a vast diplomatic & spy network. Loyalty is the core value of the nation. 

In the Hassan Dynasty, [[Aasimar]] are viewed as paragons, beyond the positive association they have elsewhere. Any lucky enough to be born as an [[Aasimar]] that travel to lands controlled by the Hassan Dynasty are able to receive an upperclass life in exchange for loyalty, and are often called upon to perform religious, traditional, and legal ceremonies. The Hassan Dynasty goes to great effort to ensure that all [[Aasimar]] are indeed following the 'righteous path', and should one rise to infamy, the Hassan Dynasty has been known to send assassins to slay those who are considered to betray their kind through misdeed. 

In the Hassan Dynasty, laws become stricter the higher up the social ladder you are. A peasant who gets into a drunken brawl will be punished less severely than a merchant, who would be punished less severely than a noble. Those of "class" are expected to act to a higher standard, and the code of laws applies accordingly. For the royal prophets, even the most minor of crimes are punished with execution. This has had the effect of causing the masses of lower class to have a substantially higher degree of trust in their leaders than in most regions, as corruption is swiftly rooted out.
