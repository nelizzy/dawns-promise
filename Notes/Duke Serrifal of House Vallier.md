# Duke Serrifal of House Vallier

%% col-start:bc:accent,sb:0 %%

%% col-break:66,stk:1,b:primary %%

A human [[nobleman]], who survived the uprisings. He read the turning of the tides quite early on, and withdrew his banners promised to the king, instead securing territory of nearby executed noblemen. 

While he has since lost most of his control of the region, he still holds onto several districts of the city, his power consolidated among the [[High Quarters of Ashport]]. 

The High Quarters also contain the only deep aquifer wells of [[Ashport]], relics of [[Tasnan]]'s golden age. During years of plentiful rain, water flows freely throughout [[Ashport]]. In times of drought, however, the city depends almost entirely upon the Duke's wells. 

He is thought of as a vile and despicable man by the people at large, viewed as a remnant of the corruption they fought and died to purge. Many crude remarks are made of being a [[Dwarves|dwarf]] at heart, such is his miserly nature. 

Despite the people's distaste, however, it is clear that he has a shrewd mind, being one of an exceedingly small number of nobles who still hold any measure of power.
