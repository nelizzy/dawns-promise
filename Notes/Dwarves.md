# Dwarves

A little under 300 years ago, the very concept of greed did not exist within the world. 

This did not mean that everything was perfect, there was still struggles for power, control, security, and a hundred other things that motivate people to take action, but the simple desire to acquire for acquisition / hoarding's sake did not exist. 

This changed when a deep dwarven mine shaft, on another continent, discovered a new material, one that they named Adamantine. A massive, perfect sphere of adamantine, miles down in the earth. 

New techniques and materials were built, capable of breaking apart the unknown metal, and began to harvest, selling it for untold fortunes. However, as they continued to break apart the Adamantine, they eventually cracked the inner shell of it, and forth oozed a brilliantly shining, golden substance, and Greed was born.

From one dwarf to the next within the mine, each dwarf was corrupted by insatiable greed, the all-consuming need for more. The corruption spread and diluted across the world, but sunk more deeply into the dwarves, being of the blood who released Greed.

While for the other races, this simply brought them to the (ooc) "normal" level of greed, for dwarves, they are at risk of [[Greed Sickness]]. 

A dwarf can smell curses, and with a touch, can determine any conditions that can lift the curse.

Dwarves are master stonecrafters, able to carve weapons and armor from stone, fortified and sharpened to perfection. They are capable of making weapons, as well as light armor, medium armor, and shields from stone with the same effectiveness, although heavier. Heavy armor is incredibly difficult to carve, and will be either far more expensive, or with heavier armor check penalties in addition to heavy weight.

![[Greed Sickness]]

Dwarves are looked upon by many with distain, being blamed for the impact of Greed. [[The Bodin Union]] is one of the few places on the continent where they are fully respected, having been originally formed from an alliance of a [[Humans|Human]] and [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] kingdom.

Dwarves are nearly impossible to completely kill without executing, being extraordinarily hardy creatures.

Additionally, dwarves breathe differently than typical creatures. While the specific molecular breakdowns are not known in world, the ooc equivalent is taking in carbon dioxide and breathing out oxygen, and suffocating in a fully oxygenated environment. Enough exists in the ambient air to breath, although in their caves and halls beneath the earth, they keep a supply of fuel for the ever burning Life Pyres to maintain a breathable atmosphere.
