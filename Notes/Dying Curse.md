# Dying Curse
When a [[Dwarves|dwarf]] dies, they may issue a dying curse on the one they blame for their death. 

The curse is automatically lifted if the [[Dwarves|dwarf]] is resurrected, and additionally, the [[Dwarves|dwarf]] may choose to put a condition upon the curse to lift it if they wish. 

The curse only ever applies to one individual, and it is more of who the [[Dwarves|dwarf]] blames for the death than necessarily the one who deals it. 

For example, a [[Dwarves|dwarf]] works for a logging company. He gets into a fight with a coworker, and the logging overseer fires the [[Dwarves|dwarf]], keeping the other employee out of a strong bias against [[Dwarves|dwarves]]. Unable to find work, the [[Dwarves|dwarf]] is evicted, starving into the poorest streets of the city, and is there confronted by a mugger, who stabs and kills the [[Dwarves|dwarf]] when he is unable to pay. 

This [[Dwarves|dwarf]] might blame the mugger for the death (as he immediately caused it), or maybe he blames the manager for his situation, for having fired him unjustly. Perhaps he blames the original coworker he fought, for having brought the issue at all. 

As a note, it isn’t a “pick and choose from the options”, but rather, “who does this particular [[Dwarves|dwarf]] genuinely blame most for their death?”
