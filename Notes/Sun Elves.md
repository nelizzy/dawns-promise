# Sun Elves

Sun Elves are, more or less, a complete match with standard Pathfinder elves, and the most numerous [[Elves|elf]] on the continent. 

Sun elves are universally vegetarian, not possessing the capability to digest meat. 

Sun Elves reach adulthood in their late 20s, living up to about 200 years old. 

Sun elves culturally make armor from specially treated groves, creating a durable ironwood. They cannot make heavy armors. 

Their weapons are primarily wood, with obsidian for cutting force, and Living Thorns, a cultivated vine with a large thorn, used for spear points.
