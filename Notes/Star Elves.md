# Star Elves

Star Elves consider only themselves to be the only true breed of Elves, and all others to be long distinct half breeds of lesser races. 

Star Elves are not born, but rather called in a ritual unknown to others. They appear in a grown body, with vast wisdom, knowledge, and skill. Star Elves consider themselves perfected mortals, and have no need to breath, eat, sleep, or drink, with immunity to disease and are unaging. 

The weakest Star Elf is vastly beyond the capability of a typical man, however their interest is exclusively in witnessing long foretold, and zealously guarded, prophecies come to pass.

A star elf has no interest in helping other races, caring none for their plights, even if easily solved. They would not cure a sick man, even if trivially capable, in the same way that a man does not provide sugar for the ants in his yard. 

Star Elves are deeply distained by most races for their arrogance, and are the least common of the elves.
