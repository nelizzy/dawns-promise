# Harath

A land of sweltering heat and tropical jungle, Harath is the ancestral home of the [[Drow]] of [[🌎 Ekoris]]. 

A dangerous land to outsiders, many who leave the cities unaccompanied become prey to the beasts that stalk the jungles. 

Cities are a mix of densely populated underground caverns, and cleared swaths of jungle above, concentrating large populations into what would barely even be considered a town in other lands. 

The vast majority of worship is to [[Outsiders]] of all sorts rather than deities. Shrines and temples to familiar deities are all but unknown in Harath, with the locals instead offering worship to outsiders, either through distant prayer or direct service to those who walk the lands. More so than other faiths, the people of Harath will offer worship to many, and do not find contradictory natures as conflicting. A powerful priestess may serve both a celestial and fiendish power, gaining strength from each in exchange for favors to both. 

Harath trades for much of their metal, with major exports being beasts of war, labor, and food.
