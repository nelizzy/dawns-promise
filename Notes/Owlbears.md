## Player Notes
- Owlbears are magically engineered war beasts bred to carry riders and to fight [^1]
- They escaped society and now breed in the wild
- They range from 9ft to 30ft in size
- Original mutation is very unstable so traits vary a lot
- Some owlbears can control minds with sound
