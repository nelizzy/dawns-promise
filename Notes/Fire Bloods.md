# Fire Bloods
The Fire Bloods are another mercenary company operating in [[Ashport]]. 

They've got a reputation for getting the job done, caring little for consequences and collateral damage in the process. 

While strongly disliked by the people of [[Ashport]], they have proven themselves effective, and have been profitable enough to pay fines as needed. 

That said, they still are in a very tenuous position within the city, considered to be one more incident from being banished or worse. 

They are infamous for highly aggressive fire magic and explosive alchemy.
