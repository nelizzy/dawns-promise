# A Faire's Ill Portents

Sponsored by Tarruk the Seer of Brindinford

This request comes from outside Ashport, from the town of Brindinford. The Baron's seer, Tarruk, has had a vision of great woe. While his visions are unclear, he believes a terrible evil will come to Brindinford, and he is uncertain if he can trust those within. He seeks assistance from those beyond the walls of Brindinford to aid.

Such visions center around a travelling faire, currently on their way towards Brindinford, a tribe of nomadic halflings that pass through once every few years with much celebration.

What fate awaits is unknown, but he assures that a great many will be in your debt, should you prevent the oncoming calamity.
