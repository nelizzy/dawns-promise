# Illithia

Once a powerful orcish nation, Illithia was brought to ruin from a disaster nearly 200 years ago. 

A major shift in the elemental balance of the resource rich archipelago caused it to be beset by countless hurricanes, earthquakes, sudden swells, and tsunamis, with a plummeting temperature killing the crops that survived. 

Now, only small clans and tribes remain, fighting for the precious little insulated territory in the region. It takes a brave or foolish captain to sail into Illithia, which has left many of the old kingdom's riches unclaimed, and the ruins of Illithia have been known to tempt many treasure hunters.
