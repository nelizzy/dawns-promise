# Leviathan

Deific tier beast asleep in the ocean, bits of it drift off as [[Leviathan Spawn]]. 

Two cults of the Leviathan: [[appeasers]] and [[awakeners]]. 

The more you give up that means something to you, the less [[Leviathan Spawn]] end up on your shores. 

In [[Tasnan]], people care about money. 


![[Leviathan Spawn]]

## Player Notes
- Bone quills forged from a piece of the Leviathan write in purple ink that visually flows (like water). [^1]
	- Using (or even handling!) anything written by Leviathan ink carries a "cost you do not want to pay".
	- Spells/rituals cast from scrolls of Leviathan make sound like "crashing waves and swelling tides" instead of speech (see [[Depth Speech]])
	- [[The Speakers]] of [[Brindinford]] say any Leviathan made artifact or spell should be destroyed immediately.
	- Examples of known Leviathan spells/artifacts include:
		- A scroll ritual spell that, when read, invokes extreme hostility and violence in nearby people. Those people's memories become warped if they survive the incident, not recognizing their own violence (though they may recognize others' violence as unprovoked!)
		- A false badge that shows a symbol of 'trusted authority' to whoever views it. It also seemed to implant memories of an identity tied to that authority in the person wielding the badge.
- Every Leviathan cult wears masks, awakeners and appeasers alike. [^2]
	- e.g. smooth masks with two glowing white eyes.
