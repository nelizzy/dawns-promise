# The Red Writ
An [[Asmodean]] collective, led by the [[Hellknight]] Signifer [[Decius Nonius]]. 

The Red Writ receives some funding from their order headquartered in the [[The Bodin Union|Bodin Union]], supplemented with selective hired work within [[Ashport]]. 

They seek to maintain order in [[Ashport]], helping the militias at times as they continue their core task - to search ancient heraldry and ancestral records, attempting to find a surviving, distant relative of the old king to put upon the throne of [[Tasnan]] once more.
