# Orcs

The flesh of an orc is naturally resilient, and bleeding wounds will close within seconds, and the loss of a limb is rarely lethal to an orc. 

The more half orcs present in an orc tribe, the more civilized it tends to be. Half orcs are capable of smelting metal. 

Metal is seen as a symbol of status among orc tribes, and an orc chief will have an array of steel weapons and plate armors, both used and displayed as trophies. Orc tribes will raid [[Humans|human]] lands for metal weapons and armor, especially heavy bludgeoning weapons for their usefulness in killing armored [[Humans|humans]].

Orcs have a short lifecycle, and are far more adaptive with each generation that other species. Orcs forced into a frozen tundra, blazing desert, or deep underground will be highly adapted to their environment in a few generations.
