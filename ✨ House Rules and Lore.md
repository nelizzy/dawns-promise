# ✨ House Rules and Lore

- 20 point buy and uses Elephant in the Room except for nonlethal damage
- Cover and firing into combat:
	- Allies within 5 ft give your enemies no cover.
	- Allies further away give enemies +2 AC instead of +4.
	- Firing into combat is −2 to hit instead of −4.
	- Precise Shot removes that −2 and stops allies counting as cover for your ranged attacks.
- +1/+2/+3 enhancements are priced separately from special abilities like flaming.
	- Potency bonuses are a trinket applied to all weapons wielded by that user
	- Special abilities like flaming, keen, or shock are weapon-bound
- [[Resurrection]] is incredibly rare.
- Optional traits, drawbacks and background skills are in use. Ask Dooms about campaign traits.
	- Adds Knowledge (Local) but removes Lingusitics.
	- Adds Heal, but only if the character already has Healing options baked in (e.g. lay of hands, kinetic healing, heal spells).
- Anyone can use scrolls and wands.
	- Scrolls do not have to be understood to be activated, but ***must*** be activated willingly (see [[Magic System and Scrolls]])
- Crits against player characters usually add effects instead of multiplying damage.
	- Sometimes still multiplies damage, depending on Dooms.
- Minion rules are in use
	- Minions do not take damage from (half on save) sources like spells. 
	- Overkill damage to a minion can overflow, but only to other nearby minions (as decided by Dooms).
-  NPC stat blocks can do things outside the normal rules, and are often homebrewed.
	- Many monsters have specific ways to be dealt with ala Witcher-style.
- Company Capital represents your company's strength in three aspects (favors, contacts, etc.)
	- Rolling a company stat is 3d10 + modfiier (modifier is separate from capital amount)
	- Military is the strength of the Company's mercenaries
	- Bureaucratic is the ability to pull legal strings
	- Espionage is information gathering and stealthy maneuvering
		- Capital can be spent to retcon preparation for a situation
- Geography is determined by elemental planar influence, not any real world physics
- [[Spirits]] are supernatural entities (not necessarily ghosts)
- [[Wellsprings]] are places of power, created by planar influence
